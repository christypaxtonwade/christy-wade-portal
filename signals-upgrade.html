The Signals Reading

A reading of where your signal actually is — right now. Not your sun sign. Not a personality type. The live pattern underneath.

Most readings tell you what you already are. This one reads the relationship between what your design carries and what is actually surfacing in you now — and names the place you are being asked to move.

It runs on three signals at once: your birth chart — what you were born carrying; your Gene Keys profile — the shadow-to-gift arc of your design; and your answers to fifty-two statements — what is alive in you today. Where the three converge is named with confidence. Where they pull apart is the edge you are standing on — the thing a single-signal reading cannot find.

What you receive

Three sections, written to your specific pattern:

  • The Field You're Walking — the terrain your life is moving through now.
  • The Register and the Frequencies Surfacing — how you are meeting it, and what is rising.
  • What the Threshold Is Asking — the move available at the edge.

Delivered to your inbox within a few minutes, as a permanent link that is yours to keep.

How it works

Enter your birth data, then answer the statements by instinct — about twelve to fifteen minutes. At the end, $44, one time. Your reading is generated and sent immediately. Lose the email, and you can recover your link anytime.
