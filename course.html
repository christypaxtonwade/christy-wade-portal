Traveling the Map

Navigating the Seasons and Thresholds of the Journey

You're a traveler on a journey that began before you existed, and you arrived without a map. But the Map exists — and in six weeks you will learn how to navigate the seasons and thresholds of your life that belong uniquely to you, passed through your lineage and connected to your purpose.

Live  ·  Small  ·  Guided by the Map's author

Join the Founding Cycle

Any Reading can tell you where you are. Then it goes quiet. Most people read it once, feel the recognition, and forget it — not from indifference, but because no one showed them the next step.

Or you've noticed it — the same emotions surfacing in your mother's life and your own, the season that keeps returning, the grief that comes without explanation. Noticing is not the same as navigating. Navigating needs a Map.

What This Is

The Navigation Map is a concept that you can feel as the evolution of your soul through the experiences of your life. Drawing on archetypal psychology, astrology, Human Design, Gene Keys, and rooted in myth and story, the Navigation Map can orient you in your path and connect you to your purpose — but in a way that the Map shifts to account for your current perspective. It's not a fixed Map that everybody travels the same way. Your journey is unique, and you can choose where you want to go. See the Architecture.

This course is Map literacy — not a Reading delivered to you, but the ability to read. Terrain, not typology; a practice, not a course binder.

Three layers, woven across six weeks: the season (your Reading)  ·  the structure (your Chart)  ·  the line (your inheritance).

The Map — thirteen Fields, three arcs, sixty-nine Subterrains. See the Architecture
