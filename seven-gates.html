The story underneath

A queen went down into the underworld on purpose. She passed through seven gates. At each one something was taken from her, and each time she asked why, she was told not to argue. She arrived at the bottom with nothing, was killed by her sister's look, and hung on a peg for three days. What happened next is the part almost no one tells: she did not climb out. Something came for her, help too small to be stopped, and she left only by handing over the one thing that had not mourned her. She rose as the other star.

That story is four thousand years old, and it is the most precise map of a woman's transformation that exists. The Seven Gates walks it, gate by gate, in plain language, with your own life as the text.

You are not lost. You are at the threshold.

The seven weeks

One gate a week, in the order the story gives them. Venus sets the calendar: she stations retrograde the day after the first gate, disappears between the two stars in the fourth week, rises as morning star in the fifth, and stations direct on the day of the seventh. Nothing about that needs to be understood for the walk to work. It is simply when it is.

What a week holds

The letter

Friday morning, before the circle, a long letter on the gate: the story told in full for that week, what the gate asks, its gift and its trap, the movement, a line for your journal.

The circle

One live call each week, Fridays at 12:00 noon Central (1 pm Eastern, 10 am Pacific, 6 pm London), on Zoom, recorded. Sixty to seventy-five minutes. The gate walked aloud, together, with the story, the week's movement, and what is actually happening in the room.

The movement

One act for the week, and only one. At the fourth gate it is a duration, not an act: three days of deciding nothing.

The deeper floor

From the fourth week, for those who want it: the inherited story, the body, and the map underneath the gates. Never required. Always there.

Who this is for

  • Women who are somewhere between who they were and who they are becoming.
  • Women whose life works but who are not sure who it is working for.
  • Women who have set something down, or had something taken, and are finding out what it costs.
  • You do not need to know astrology, mythology, or anything about this work. You need to be standing at a gate.

It is not for a woman who needs to be convinced that something is changing in her. It is not therapy, and it does not replace it. It is a walk, in company, along a road that has been walked before.

Questions

What if I can't make the live call? Every call is recorded and sent the same day. The letter and the movement carry the week; the call is where it is said aloud.

Do I need to know the myth, or astrology? No. The story is told to you across the seven weeks, in plain language. Venus sets the dates; you do not have to follow her to walk.

What if I am at Gate Five and the walk starts at Gate One? Everyone walks all seven in order. The gates you have already passed look different from further along, and the one you are standing at arrives when it arrives. Where you are does not change where we start.

Is this therapy? No, and it is not a substitute for it. It is a reflective walk in company, and it will not ask you to disclose anything you do not choose to.

Refunds? Through the end of week one, in full, no questions. After that, no; the walk depends on the circle staying whole.
