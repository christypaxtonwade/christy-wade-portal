The Mythic Tapestry

Archetypal Substrate · Chosen Resonance · The Canopy of Sky

Beneath the chart — beneath the placements and the transits — sits a tapestry of figures who keep returning. Not because the chart contains them, but because the lineage of the work recognizes them. The Tapestry names them.

Voice review · placeholder copy below — Christy to revise

The chart names the structure: which Fields are active, which Subterrains have weight, where the lineage's compression is sitting in this body. The Tapestry names the figures who move through that structure — the archetypal operations that illuminate what the placements alone cannot.

These are not deities to be worshipped or invoked. They are operations — described patterns of how a frequency moves when a body carries it. Persephone moves in one way. Hecate moves in another. Inanna in a third. When a chart shows Field X active and a Lineage carries the Wound That Will Not Close, knowing which archetypal figure moves through that frequency is what makes the reading actionable.

The Tapestry is the substrate of Sky's offerings. Every Sovereign Signal reading walks through it; every Lineage Session writes against it; every Mirror engagement deepens within it. What follows is the public face — the catalog of archetypes documented so far. The work itself happens inside the offerings, where these figures get named by their specific resonance to your lineage.
