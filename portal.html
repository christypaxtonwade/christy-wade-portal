Tier Reference ›

Tier 1Free · Public · LIVE

The Map as initiation. Visual overview at /map — 13 Fields named, three-arc structure, brief Conceptual-register descriptions. Portal view shows Map overview only. No Fieldguide content. Granted to every client who has crossed any threshold (discovery call, single session, intake completed).

Tier 2Free · Email-gated · NOT in portal

The Architecture PDF — 24-page designed download. Delivered via Kit subscription form at /map#subscribe. Lives outside the portal entirely; never gated here. Listed for orientation only.

Tier 3$88 launch · $111 standard · PDF-only · LAUNCHING 15 JULY 2026

The Fieldguide. All 276 passages (13 Fields × 69 Subterrains × 4 registers) at client-facing depth. Astrological correspondences and Mythic Tapestry profiles woven in. Delivered as PDF only — not rendered live in the portal or on the website. Sent or sold to: Fieldguide purchasers, Cohort participants (post-15-July), Sovereign Signal clients at discretion, all Mirror Work clients. Delivery happens outside the portal.

Tier 4Lineage Session $444 / Mirror Work $1,888 · LIVE

The Ancestral Weaving. Personal lineage research and transmission — curated ancestors written across five layers (Terroir, Unresolved, Thread, Lineage, Sigil), frequency-tagged to specific Fields and Subterrains. Portal view: their Weaving rendered, each ancestor with full layers and frequency tags. Granted to: Lineage Session clients (12-ancestor starter), Weaving Extension clients (+8 ancestors), Mirror Work clients (Weaving deepens across 3 months).

Tier 5Bundled into Lineage / $244 update / Mirror capstone · LIVE

The Convergence. 12-section pattern analysis generated from a client's Weaving frequency data, plus 2-page summary PDF, plus a reading session in which Christy walks them through the analysis. Portal view: history strip of all their Convergences, current Convergence rendered in full, PDF download. Granted to: Lineage Session clients (first Convergence ~2 weeks after session), Convergence Update clients ($244 for returning Weaving-holders), Mirror Work clients (capstone in month 3).

Tier reflects access, not how someone arrived at it. A friend comp'd into Tier 4 still sees a Tier 4 portal — the Relationship field on each client captures the revenue/audit fact separately. Access is granted per-flag on each client's detail page.
